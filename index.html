What we do

01

Aqua Parks & Water Slides

Complete aqua parks with body slides, tube slides, family rafts and towers, plus rehabilitation and maintenance of existing parks.

02

Kids' Pool Slides & Splash Pads

Colourful water play structures, mini slides, tipping buckets and splash pads designed for safe fun in children's pools.

Olympic and competition pools, club and compound pools, and private villa pools: structure, waterproofing, tiling, filtration and plant rooms.

Who we are

Two specialist teams. One stronger partner.

Demas for Industrial & Construction Solutions built its name on aqua parks, swimming pools, fountains and kids' areas. Blue for Construction & Supplies built its name on sports surfaces, shades and site furniture.

Both companies have now come together under one name: Partners for Construction. Same people, same experience, now offering every service under one contract and one point of contact.

  • Turnkey delivery: design, supply, civil works & installation
  • Trusted by top developers, resorts and sporting clubs
  • Projects across Egypt and abroad (Maldives)
  • Maintenance & rehabilitation of existing facilities
